## 1. Mata Uang Resmi Xuantian World

Di Xuantian World, transaksi fana dan kultivator dipisahkan secara tegas:

### 1.1 Mudarat/Sirkulasi Mata Uang Fana (Mortal Currency)
* **Tael Tembaga (Copper Tael)**
* **Tael Perak (Silver Tael):** 1 Tael Perak = 100 Tael Tembaga
* **Tael Emas (Gold Tael):** 1 Tael Emas = 100 Tael Perak

### 1.2 Mata Uang Kultivator (Spiritual Stones)
* **Batu Spirit Kualitas Rendah (Low-Grade Spirit Stone):** 1 Low Spirit Stone = 10 Tael Emas
* **Batu Spirit Kualitas Sedang (Mid-Grade Spirit Stone):** 1 Mid Spirit Stone = 100 Low Spirit Stones
* **Batu Spirit Kualitas Tinggi (High-Grade Spirit Stone):** 1 High Spirit Stone = 100 Mid Spirit Stones
* **Batu Spirit Purba (Ancient Spirit Stone):** 1 Ancient Spirit Stone = 100 High Spirit Stones
